MarTech Stack ROI Audit

What it does

You list every tool in your marketing stack, who runs it, who depends on it, and which of its features your teams actually use versus just pay for. The audit then finds features that more than one tool can do and recommends what to keep, right-size, consolidate or cut, with an estimated annual saving for each call.

How it works

  • 1. Stack inventory. Add tools by hand, import a CSV, or load the sample stack. For each one: annual cost, renewal date, licensed vs. active seats, business criticality, how well it integrates with your core stack, an admin owner and a champion.
  • 2. Feature comparison. A matrix shows which tools use or merely include each feature, which features are paid for more than once, and the recommended home for each.
  • 3. Audit results. Headline spend and savings, a recommendation and plain-language reason for every tool, and a list of the champions to consult before anything changes.
The idea behind it

Hidden redundancy lives in features a tool includes but nobody uses, like a chat widget already inside your automation platform while you pay for a separate chat tool. So every feature is tagged as used or just available, and both count toward overlap.

How it decides

  • Value score (0–100): 40% seat utilization, 35% business criticality, 25% integration with the core stack.
  • Consolidate when most of what a tool is used for is already covered by a higher-value tool that is staying, and the tool isn't high-criticality. If adoption is strong, it's flagged for review instead.
  • Cut when the value score is low and criticality is low, listing any feature only that tool provides so nobody is surprised.
  • Right-size when seat utilization is under target: drop to active seats plus a growth buffer.
  • People first: any change flags the tool's champion, so the team that depends on it is part of the decision, not just the admin.

The thresholds are adjustable in Settings, and results update immediately. First-year net subtracts a one-time switching cost for consolidations.

Your data stays with you

The tool runs entirely in your browser. Your stack is saved locally on your device, nothing is uploaded, and you can export it to CSV or print the report at any time.

About the sample data

Every tool name, cost, seat count, renewal date and savings figure the tool opens with is a made-up placeholder for demonstration. None of it reflects a real company's tech stack, pricing or contracts.
